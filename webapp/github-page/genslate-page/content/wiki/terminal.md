> [!IMPORTANT]
> Terminal is **planned**. This page describes the design it is being built to; details may change
> before the first release. The picture on its [app page](/apps/terminal/) is a live preview built
> from the design kit.

Terminal is a fast, modern terminal that feels at home next to the rest of the suite and runs from
your GENSLATE drive like everything else.

## Planned features

- **Tabs and splits.** Split any pane horizontally or vertically, move focus with the keyboard, and
  drag tabs between windows.
- **Every shell.** PowerShell, cmd, bash, zsh, fish and WSL profiles, detected automatically, with
  per-profile start folders and environment variables.
- **Nord, tuned.** Polar Night and Snow Storm palettes where every ANSI colour is checked for contrast.
- **Command palette.** Every action behind <kbd>mod</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>P</kbd>.
- **Portable history.** Profiles, history and layouts stay in the suite folder.
- **Launcher integration.** `/open terminal`, and "open a terminal here" from Explorer.

## Settings

Like every GENSLATE app, Terminal will keep its settings in
`other/config/genslate/terminal/config.toml` and `keybindings.toml`, hot-reloaded on save.
