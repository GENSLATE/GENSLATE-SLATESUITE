GENSLATE is a suite of desktop apps for Windows, macOS and Linux that share one design language, one
settings format and one folder you can carry anywhere. The **Launcher** is its home: a start menu for
your portable apps, files and tools.

## Why GENSLATE

- **Portable.** Every app runs from the folder you unzip it into — a USB drive, a synced folder, a
  second disk. Settings, caches, logs and your own files live beside the apps, not scattered across
  the computer.
- **Beautiful and calm.** The apps use the official [Nord](https://www.nordtheme.com) palette in two
  first-class themes, **Polar Night** (dark) and **Snow Storm** (light), with the refinement of macOS
  and the density of VS Code.
- **Fast and small.** Each app is a native [Tauri 2](https://v2.tauri.app) program with a Rust core.
  It uses the webview your operating system already has instead of bundling a browser engine.
- **Yours.** No accounts, no analytics. Settings are plain, commented TOML files you can read, edit
  and back up.

**Preview** apps build from source today. **Planned** apps are designed — the pictures on this site
are live previews made with the design kit — and arrive one by one. See the [roadmap](/docs/roadmap/).

## Also runs your other portable apps

The Launcher also lists apps in the [PortableApps.com](https://portableapps.com) format and
[portapps](https://portapps.io) apps, each in their own tab, so one menu covers everything on your
drive.
